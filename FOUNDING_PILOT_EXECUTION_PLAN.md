## Founding Pilot Execution Plan (FPEP)
### First-Phase Institutional Implementation, School Selection Rubrics, Teacher Frameworks, & Impact Verification Protocols

---

## EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE & PILOT MISSION

The **Bakenye Kids Founding Pilot Execution Plan (FPEP)** defines the operational execution roadmap for the inaugural institutional deployment of Bakenye Kids across pioneer primary schools and community learning centers in the Lake Kyoga basin of Uganda.

As the first live-environment deployment of the platform, the Founding Pilot bridges research validation and long-term national scaling. FPEP provides concrete operational guidelines, school selection scoring rubrics, classroom integration schedules, teacher success frameworks, student learning assessment protocols, community engagement workflows, data collection schemas, and success benchmarks to establish a proven model for digital indigenous language education.

# 2. PILOT SELECTION FRAMEWORK

Pioneer primary schools are selected and prioritized using a weighted **Pilot Eligibility Score (PES)** calculation:

$$\text{Pilot Eligibility Score (PES)} = (C_1 \times 0.25) + (C_2 \times 0.25) + (C_3 \times 0.20) + (C_4 \times 0.15) + (C_5 \times 0.15)$$

### Criteria Breakdown & Scoring Rubric (1–5 Scale)

| Criterion Code | Selection Criterion | Description & Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`C_1`** | **Cultural Relevance (25%)** | Located in core Bakenye-speaking communities in Lake Kyoga basin. |
| **`C_2`** | **School Commitment (25%)** | Headteacher and primary literacy staff committed to 20-min daily usage. |
| **`C_3`** | **Teacher Availability (20%)** | At least 2 certified primary literacy teachers available for onboarding. |
| **`C_4`** | **Secure Hardware Storage (15%)** | Lockable storage cabinet and secure room available in headteacher's office. |
| **`C_5`** | **Accessibility & Field Access (15%)** | Accessible by field officers for monthly OTG USB updates and support. |

- **Selection Threshold:** Schools scoring **PES ≥ 4.0** are designated as **Founding Pioneer Schools**.

# 3. PILOT DEPLOYMENT TIMELINE

The 12-week Founding Pilot is executed across five structured operational phases:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    PILOT DEPLOYMENT TIMELINE                    │
├─────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Weeks 1–2   │ Phase 1: Preparation & Hardware Provisioning     │
├─────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Weeks 3–4   │ Phase 2: School Onboarding & Teacher Certification│
├─────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Weeks 5–9   │ Phase 3: Active Classroom Usage & Rotation        │
├─────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Weeks 10–11 │ Phase 4: Impact Assessment & Data Collection      │
├─────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Week 12     │ Phase 5: Founding Impact Report & Stakeholder Sync│
└─────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Phase Deliverables & Owners
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–2):** Provision 50 tablets with pre-seeded Room DB; deliver charging cabinets. *(Owner: Technical Support Lead)*.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 3–4):** Train 30 teachers across Modules 1–5; execute school MOUs. *(Owner: Educational Coordinator)*.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 5–9):** Execute 20-min daily learning sessions 3 days/week in 10 pioneer schools. *(Owner: Field Operations Lead)*.
- **Phase 4 (Weeks 10–11):** Administer 24-hour vocabulary retention tests; export local DB logs. *(Owner: Research Analyst)*.
- **Phase 5 (Week 12):** Publish the Founding Impact Report and present findings to BLCC Council and MoES. *(Owner: Pilot Director)*.

# 6. STUDENT LEARNING MEASUREMENT

Vocabulary learning is measured systematically using a 4-phase testing protocol:

```
[Phase 1: Baseline Pre-Test] ──► [Phase 2: Active Guided Learning Session]
                                                 │
                                                 ▼
[Phase 4: 24h Delayed Recall] ◄── [Phase 3: Immediate Post-Test Quiz]
```

- **Baseline Score:** Unassisted identification of picture cards before starting a lesson.
- **Immediate Gain:** Matching activity score immediately following flashcard exploration.
- **24-Hour Recall:** Surprise 24-hour delayed recall quiz when the child re-opens the app next day.
- **Target Standard:** **≥ 80% 24-Hour Vocabulary Retention Gain** over baseline.

# 7. COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Community participation is nurtured through four active engagement touchpoints:

1. **Parent Briefings:** Community meetings explaining the zero-cost offline app, privacy protections, and home practice support.
2. **Elder Dedication Ceremonies:** Formal blessing of the Bakenye language assets by BLCC Council Elders prior to school launch.
3. **School Heritage Days:** Monthly community showcases where children recite Bakenye folklore stories learned in the app to parents and elders.
4. **Community Feedback Box:** Physical feedback forms available at headteacher offices for community suggestions.

# 10. FOUNDING IMPACT REPORT STRUCTURE

Upon concluding Week 12, the Pilot Director publishes the official **Founding Pilot Impact Report**:

1. **Executive Summary:** Overall pilot outcomes and regional expansion recommendation.
2. **Pioneer Schools Breakdown:** Profiles of 10 participating primary schools and student demographics.
3. **Educational Retention Analysis:** Pre-test vs. post-test vocabulary learning curves across cohorts.
4. **Cultural & Elder Audit Certification:** Final sign-off record from BLCC Elders Council.
5. **Technical & Device Performance:** Battery usage, startup latencies, and offline reliability logs.
6. **Next Steps for Regional Scaling:** Roadmap for Phase 2 expansion to 75 primary schools.
