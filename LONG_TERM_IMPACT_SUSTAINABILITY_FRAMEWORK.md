# BAKENYE KIDS PLATFORM
## Long-Term Impact & Sustainability Framework (LISF)
### Institutional Governance, Financial Resilience, Cultural Archive Preservation, & Multi-Decadal Continuity Strategy

---

## EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE & INSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE

The **Bakenye Kids Long-Term Impact & Sustainability Framework (LISF)** establishes the permanent operational, financial, technical, cultural, and governance architecture required to sustain the Bakenye Digital Language Platform across generations.

Designed to transition Bakenye Kids from a software initiative into a permanent digital heritage institution in Uganda, LISF ensures that indigenous linguistic knowledge, folklore, and cultural traditions are preserved, updated, protected, and transmitted to future generations through offline-first digital learning.

### Authority & Decision Rules
- **Cultural Authority (BLCC Elders Council):** Retains absolute veto power over language orthography, audio pronunciations, cultural representation, and story authenticity.
- **Educational Authority (Ministry & Educators):** Governs pedagogical alignment, age suitability, classroom integration, and teacher training.
- **Technical Authority (Engineering Directorate):** Governs platform stability, Android compatibility, database security, and offline distribution pipelines.
- **Operational Authority (Executive Management):** Governs financial administration, school logistics, grant reporting, and partnership development.

# 4. CONTENT PRESERVATION STRATEGY

### 4.1 Digital Heritage Preservation Architecture
- **Raw Audio Master Archive:** All native elder recordings preserved in uncompressed 24-bit 96kHz WAV format stored in redundant off-site cold storage.
- **Phonetic & Semantic Metadata:** Every vocabulary term indexed with International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) transcriptions, English/Luganda translations, dialectal notes, and elder attribution.
- **Digital Rights & Open Heritage:** All cultural content is held in trust by the BLCC Elders Council under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) license.

# 5. TECHNOLOGY SUSTAINABILITY

### 5.1 Long-Term Application Maintenance
1. **Android Forward-Compatibility:** Maintain compliance with current Android API targets (API 21 to 35+) through annual Jetpack Compose dependency updates.
2. **Offline-First Zero-Server Architecture:** Pure local SQLite database schema ensures the app remains fully playable even if central web servers are offline or decommissioned.
3. **Hardware Lifecycle Management:** School tablet inventories refreshed on a 3-year rolling cycle with local battery replacement buffer stocks.

# 10. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER & CONTINUITY

To guarantee seamless institutional continuity across leadership transitions:
1. **Engineering Documentation:** All codebase architectures, Room database schemas, and build configurations maintained in the workspace repository.
2. **Cultural Knowledge Handover:** Junior linguists paired with BLCC Elders to learn recording protocols and orthography validation.
3. **Succession Protocol:** Executive leadership positions require a 6-month shadow period prior to full handover.

---

# 11. ANNUAL REVIEW PROCESS

Every December, the Governing Council conducts a 2-day Annual Review:
1. **Impact Audit:** Review annual student retention metrics and active school adoption logs.
2. **Cultural Audit:** Review new language assets added to the digital repository.
3. **Financial Audit:** Review endowment health, grant allocations, and MoES funding lines.
4. **Strategic Roadmap Update:** Publish updated 3-year rolling strategy document for stakeholders.
