# BAKENYE KIDS PLATFORM
## Beta Operations & Learning Intelligence Plan (BOLIP)
### Post-Release Operations, Learning Analytics, School Partner Workflows, & Version 1.0 Graduation Framework

---

## EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE & OPERATIONS PHILOSOPHY

The **Bakenye Kids Beta Operations & Learning Intelligence Plan (BOLIP)** defines the operational framework for running, monitoring, analyzing, and continually refining the Bakenye Digital Language Platform throughout its public Beta phase.

Moving beyond static release readiness, BOLIP establishes real-time operational rhythms, learning intelligence feedback loops, classroom coordinator workflows, content pipeline governance, and a data-driven experimentation engine. The goal of this phase is to continuously learn from children in real-world educational settings, optimize vocabulary retention, and prepare Bakenye Kids for sustainable national deployment.

### 1.2 Stakeholder Role Responsibilities

- **Product Lead:** Owns feature prioritization, product roadmap decisions, and Version 1.0 graduation gates.

- **Teacher Coordinators:** Facilitates primary school integration, device rotation, and teacher feedback collection.
- **Cultural Elders (BLCC):** Governs linguistic accuracy, traditional storytelling, and audio pronunciation integrity.
- **Research & Analytics Lead:** Analyzes vocabulary learning intelligence, calculates retention curves, and runs A/B experiments.

# 3. LEARNING INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK

### 3.1 Measuring Genuine Child Vocabulary Acquisition

To prove that screen time translates directly into language retention, the Learning Intelligence Engine evaluates vocabulary acquisition across three temporal checkpoints:

$$\text{Learning Improvement Score (LIS)} = \left( \frac{\text{24h Recall Score} - \text{Baseline Score}}{\text{Total Lesson Terms}} \right) \times 100$$

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              TEMPORAL LEARNING CHECKPOINT MATRIX                │
├───────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Checkpoint Phase  │ Measurement Mechanism                       │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Checkpoint 1:     │ Unassisted picture identification before   │
│ Baseline          │ starting a new lesson                       │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Checkpoint 2:     │ Interactive matching activity immediately    │
│ Immediate Gain    │ following flashcard exposure                │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Checkpoint 3:     │ Surprise 24-hour delayed recall quiz when    │
│ 24-Hour Retention │ child re-opens the app next day             │
└───────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

# 5. SCHOOL PARTNER OPERATIONS

### 5.1 Primary School Support Workflow

To ensure seamless integration into partner primary schools across the Lake Kyoga basin, operations follow a standardized school lifecycle:

```
[1. Teacher Onboarding Workshop] ──► [2. Offline Tablet Provisioning]
                                                  │
                                                  ▼
[4. Monthly Usage Audit] ◄── [3. Classroom Rotation (3x / Week)]
```

1. **Teacher Onboarding:** 2-hour practical training session equipping teachers to integrate Bakenye Kids into morning literacy hours.
2. **Device Management:** Offline tablets provided in ruggedized protective cases with charging stations.
3. **Classroom Usage Reporting:** Monthly summary cards provided to school headteachers detailing total vocabulary words mastered per class.
