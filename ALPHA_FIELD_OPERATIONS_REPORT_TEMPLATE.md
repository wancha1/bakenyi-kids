## 5. USER EXPERIENCE (UX) FINDINGS & DISCOVERIES

### 5.1 Positive UX Discoveries
1. **Audio First Autonomy:** Pre-literate children (Cohort A) successfully completed activities by relying exclusively on native audio speaker buttons without reading English subtitles.
2. **Slow Mode Adoption:** 48% of children actively toggled the `0.75x` slow audio button when learning multi-syllable phrases like *Osiibye otya*.

### 5.2 Observed UX Friction Points
- **Friction Item 1 (Locked Node Feedback):** Children tapping locked nodes on the World Map received zero visual feedback, causing repeated taps and brief confusion.
  - *Priority Risk Score (PRS):* **12.0 (High Priority - P1)**.
  - *Recommended Action:* Add a shaking animation and soft padlock chime when a locked node is tapped.

## 11. LESSONS LEARNED

1. **Audio Playback Speed Matters:** Providing a slow audio toggle (`0.75x`) significantly boosts confidence and pronunciation accuracy for younger children.
2. **Offline Resilience Builds Trust:** Families and schools in Lake Kyoga deeply appreciated that the app worked flawlessly without requiring internet access or data purchases.
