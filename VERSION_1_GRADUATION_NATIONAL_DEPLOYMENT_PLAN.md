---

## EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE & NATIONAL PURPOSE

The **Bakenye Kids Version 1.0 Graduation & National Deployment Plan (V1GDP)** defines the strategic transition of the Bakenye Digital Language Platform from a validated Beta release into a permanent, national-scale digital heritage and early-childhood education infrastructure in Uganda.

Developed through rigorous field testing across primary schools and households in the Lake Kyoga basin, Bakenye Kids combines modern Jetpack Compose software engineering, 100% offline-first architecture, scientifically verified vocabulary learning workflows, and strict governance by the **Bakenye Language & Culture Council (BLCC)**.

This document establishes the official graduation criteria, feature scope, 3-phase national rollout roadmap, school deployment model, content pipeline expansion, long-term governance structure, and impact measurement framework required to preserve indigenous languages and empower young learners at national scale.

# 4. SCHOOL IMPLEMENTATION MODEL

### 4.1 End-to-End Primary School Lifecycle

```
[1. Ministry & District Approval] ──► [2. Teacher Orientation Workshop]
                                                │
                                                ▼
[4. Monthly Analytics Audit] ◄── [3. Ruggedized Tablet Deployment]
```

1. **Teacher Training & Onboarding:** 3-hour practical certification equipping primary school instructors to run 20-minute daily language learning modules.
2. **Device Management:** Tablets housed in protective, water-resistant charging cabinets with lockable storage in the school headteacher's office.
3. **Classroom Integration:** Integrated into morning literacy circles (3 days per week) as a supplementary digital learning tool.
4. **Technical Support & Updates:** Local field officers conduct monthly visits with USB/OTG update drives to synchronize telemetry and update software offline.

```
└──────────────┘    └──────────────┘    └──────────────┘    └──────────────┘
```

### Governance Responsibilities
- **BLCC Elders Board:** Retains absolute veto power over language orthography, audio pronunciations, and cultural heritage representation.
- **Ministry of Education (MoES):** Authorizes primary school curriculum integration and district deployment access.
- **Technical Engineering Team:** Maintains codebase, build stability, offline database migrations, and security patches.
- **Product & Operations Team:** Coordinates field logistics, user research, hardware distribution, and grant reporting.
