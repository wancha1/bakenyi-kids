# BAKENYE DIGITAL LANGUAGE FOUNDATION (BDLF)
## Master Charter & 100-Year Operational Framework
### Institutional Governance, Perpetual Digital Preservation, Open Source Ecosystem, and Global Expansion for Indigenous Language Revitalization

---

## Executive Summary

The **Bakenye Digital Language Foundation (BDLF)** is an independent, non-profit, public-benefit foundation established to ensure the perpetual preservation, revitalisation, educational transmission, and digital sovereignty of the Bakenye language and culture. Designed to endure for 50–100 years and beyond, BDLF provides institutional governance, open-source technology, research infrastructure, and community leadership to safeguard endangered African languages across generations.

This document serves as the **Master Foundation Charter & Long-Term Operational Framework**, providing the legal, strategic, organizational, and technological foundation for presentation to international bodies (UNESCO, African Union), ministries of education, academic research partners, and philanthropic trusts.

---

## SECTION 1: FOUNDATION CHARTER

### 1.1 Mission Statement
To protect, preserve, and revitalize the Bakenye language and African indigenous languages through immersive child education, community-owned digital technology, intergenerational storytelling, and open research archives.

### 1.2 Long-Term Vision (100-Year Horizon)
A world where every indigenous child has the right and technology to learn, speak, celebrate, and expand their mother tongue seamlessly in the digital age, ensuring zero language extinction across the African continent.

### 1.3 Core Values
1. **Indigenous Sovereignty:** Cultural communities maintain absolute ownership and authority over their language, stories, and heritage data.
2. **Intergenerational Bridge:** Technology serves as a humble conduit connecting elders' ancestral wisdom with children's playful discovery.
3. **Public Benefit & Zero Exploitation:** Free from commercial advertising, user tracking, or monetization of children's data.
4. **Open Science & Open Access:** Educational engines and digital preservation standards are open-source and freely accessible to humanity.
5. **Quality & Authenticity:** Uncompromising linguistic accuracy verified by elder consensus and academic scholarship.

### 1.4 Public Benefit Statement
BDLF operates exclusively as a public-benefit foundation. All software, content schemas, research outputs, and educational apps created or managed by BDLF are dedicated to the public good under non-commercial, community-friendly open licenses.

| Governance Body | Primary Responsibility | Voting Right / Weight | Succession Mechanism |

| **Board of Trustees (5-7)** | Fiduciary oversight, strategic policy, legal stewardship | Executive majority | 3-year staggered terms; elected by Council consensus |
| **Cultural Elders Council (5-9)** | Final veto & approval on cultural & linguistic content | Veto power on content & orthography | Designated by clan lineage & elder nomination |

| **Education Advisory Board (5)** | Pedagogical standards, child safety, curriculum alignment | Advisory vote | 2-year rotating academic appointments |
| **Research Advisory Board (5)** | Ethno-linguistic research, AI ethics, grant programs | Advisory vote | Appointed by university research consortium |

| **International Advisory Panel** | Global advocacy, UNESCO coordination, cross-border expansion | Non-voting advisory | Invited global experts in language preservation |

### 2.2 Conflict Resolution & Orthography Arbitration
1. **Tier 1 (Linguistic Dispute):** Language Standards Committee reviews phonetic evidence.
2. **Tier 2 (Cultural Protocol Dispute):** Cultural Elders Council holds oral hearing; decisions require 75% elder consensus.
3. **Tier 3 (Institutional Dispute):** Board of Trustees conducts formal arbitration adhering to Ugandan legal and international NGO standards.

## SECTION 6: COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE & CONTRIBUTOR SAFEGUARDS

### 6.1 Contributor Code of Ethics & Royalty Protection
- **Community Attribution:** All elders, storytellers, and singers are credited in perpetuity in app metadata and research archives.
- **Consent Protocol:** Prior Informed Consent (PIC) forms signed and archived digitally for every audio and video recording.
- **Non-Commercial Exclusivity:** Contributed cultural assets cannot be sold or licensed for commercial entertainment or corporate advertising.

---

## SECTION 7: OPEN SOURCE & OPEN CONTENT STRATEGY

### 7.1 Dual-Licensing Architecture
- **Software Codebase (Android & Tools):** Licensed under **Apache 2.0** or **MIT License** to maximize developer adoption.
- **Cultural Content & Assets (Audio, Stories, Art):** Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)** to protect cultural integrity against commercial exploitation.

## SECTION 8: LONG-TERM RESEARCH PROGRAM

### 8.1 Research Focus Areas
1. **Ethno-Linguistic Documentation:** Archiving tonal variations, archaic proverbs, and specialized fishing/canoeing terminology.
2. **Offline Edge AI Speech Recognition:** Low-resource acoustic modeling for African tonal languages without internet access.
3. **Child Immersion Psychology:** Empirical studies measuring mother-tongue vocabulary retention using gamified spatial maps.
