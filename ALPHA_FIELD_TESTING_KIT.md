# BAKENYE KIDS PLATFORM
## Alpha Field Testing Kit (AFK)
### Operational Research Protocols, Consent Forms, Observation Tools, & Evaluation Rubrics

---

## EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE & RESEARCH PURPOSE

The **Bakenye Kids Alpha Field Testing Kit (AFK)** provides all ready-to-use research instruments, consent documentation, observation forms, vocabulary testing protocols, and feedback rubrics required to execute field testing sessions with children, parents, teachers, and cultural elders in the Lake Kyoga region.

This toolkit ensures that every piece of field data collected is objective, child-safe, COPPA-compliant, and directly actionable for software engineering and pedagogical refinement.

# 1. CHILD PARTICIPANT PROTOCOL

### 1.1 Welcome Script & Orientation (Spoken by Facilitator)

> **Facilitator Script (English / Local Translation):**
> "Hello! My name is [Researcher Name]. Thank you so much for spending time with us today. 
> 
> Today, we are playing with a new game on this tablet called **Bakenye Kids**. It is made to help children learn words and stories in Bakenye. 
> 
> Before we start, I want you to know a few super important things:
> 1. **You are NOT taking a school test.** You cannot make a mistake. There are no wrong answers!
> 2. **We are testing the app, NOT you.** If something in the game is hard to find or confusing, that is the game's fault, not yours. You are helping us fix the game!
> 3. **You are in charge.** If you want to take a break, skip a lesson, or stop playing at any time, just tell me. You won't get into trouble, and nobody will be upset with you.
> 
> Are you ready to explore Bakenye Kids with me?"

### 1.2 Session Rules & Safety Standards
- **Rule 1 (Child Ergonomics):** The tablet must be seated on a stable table or held comfortably in the child's lap. The child should never be forced to hold a heavy tablet awkwardly.
- **Rule 2 (Non-Interference):** The researcher must observe quietly without tapping buttons for the child or answering "What do I press next?". Allow up to 15 seconds of hesitation before providing minimal verbal prompts.
- **Rule 3 (Child Comfort First):** If a child shows signs of frustration, eye strain, or disinterest, pause the session immediately and offer a break or water.

# 4. CHILD INTERVIEW QUESTIONS (AGE-STRATIFIED)

### 4.1 Cohort A: Ages 5–7 (Pre-literate / Early Learners)
1. *"Show me your favourite button on this screen!"*
2. *"Did you like hearing the voices say the Bakenye words? Was it easy to hear?"*
3. *"How did you feel when the gold stars popped up on the screen?"*
4. *"If you could play with this game again tomorrow, would you want to?"*

### 4.2 Cohort B: Ages 8–10 (Primary School Learners)
1. *"Which lesson did you like best: Greetings, Family, or Lake Animals?"*
2. *"Was there any picture or word that was confusing or hard to understand?"*
3. *"Did the slow voice button (0.75x) help you hear how to say the word better?"*
4. *"What would you add to make this game even more fun for your friends at school?"*

### 4.3 Cohort C: Ages 11–13 (Youth Ambassadors)
1. *"How accurate were the Bakenye pronunciations compared to how your parents and elders speak at home?"*
2. *"Did you find the game too easy, too hard, or just right?"*
3. *"Would you prefer reading the stories by yourself or listening to the audio narration?"*
4. *"Do you feel proud using an app that teaches your native Bakenye language?"*

# 5. VOCABULARY LEARNING ASSESSMENT

### 5.1 Three-Phase Assessment Protocol & Scoring Formula

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              VOCABULARY RETENTION EVALUATION PROTOCOL           │
├───────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Phase             │ Procedure                                   │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Pre-Test       │ Show picture cards (no text/audio); ask:    │
│    (Baseline)     │ "What is the Bakenye word for this?"        │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Post-Test      │ Administer immediately after child completes│
│    (Immediate)    │ Lesson 1 (10 minutes of play).              │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Delayed Test   │ Re-test 24 hours later using identical      │
│    (24 Hours)     │ picture card prompts.                       │
└───────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Scoring Formula
$$\text{Vocabulary Retention Score (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Post-Test Correct Items} - \text{Baseline Correct Items}}{\text{Total Items Tested (e.g., 5)}} \right) \times 100$$

```
BUG ID: BUG_20260801_01
Device Model: Tecno Spark 8 (2GB RAM)
Android OS Version: Android 11 (API 30)
Severity: High (P1)
Screen / Component: Audio Flashcard Screen

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Launch App in Airplane Mode.
2. Select World 1 ➔ Lesson 1.
3. Tap speaker button 5 times rapidly.

Expected Result: Audio plays latest clip cleanly without freezing.
Actual Result: Audio playback stutters, and UI lags for 2 seconds.
Suggested Fix: Enforce single-instance audio queue in ExoPlayer manager.
```

### 9.2 UX Friction Log Entry Template
```
UX ISSUE ID: UX_20260801_02
Screen: World Map Screen
Target User Group: Cohort A (Ages 5–7)
Friction Severity: Medium (P2)

Observed Behavior:
Child repeatedly tapped locked Node 2 and looked confused when nothing happened.

UX Diagnostic:
The padlock icon is too small (20dp), and there is zero sound or visual feedback when tapping a locked node.

Suggested UX Fix:
Increase lock icon size to 32dp and add a gentle shaking animation + soft "locked" sound chime when tapped.
```
